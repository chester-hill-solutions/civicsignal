## Thursday, May 28

**ENVI presented its freshwater report and asked the government to respond.** Recommendations 50 and 52 address long-term support for Indigenous freshwater monitoring and consistent data collection across governments. These are committee proposals. [House Journals EN](https://www.ourcommons.ca/DocumentViewer/en/45-1/house/sitting-124/journals) · [FR](https://www.noscommunes.ca/DocumentViewer/fr/45-1/chambre/seance-124/journaux) · [Recommendations EN](https://www.ourcommons.ca/DocumentViewer/en/45-1/ENVI/report-5/page-21) · [FR](https://www.noscommunes.ca/DocumentViewer/fr/45-1/ENVI/rapport-5/page-21) · [House debates EN](https://www.ourcommons.ca/DocumentViewer/en/45-1/house/sitting-124/hansard#Int-13528442) · [FR](https://www.noscommunes.ca/DocumentViewer/fr/45-1/chambre/seance-124/debats#Int-13528442)
