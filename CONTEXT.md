# CivicSignal

CivicSignal is a policy research and monitoring platform for policy analysts, political staffers, and government relations teams.

## Language

**Source record**  
A citable unit from a published source, such as a provision, speech, bill, vote, or news article.

**Member**  
A person who serves or served in the House of Commons.

**Sitting**  
A meeting of the House of Commons recorded in its proceedings.

**Hansard speech**  
One speaking turn in the published House of Commons debates, including questions and replies.

**Committee intervention**  
One speaking turn in the published transcript of a House committee meeting.

**Bill**  
A proposal for legislation identified within a Parliament and session.

**Subject of debate**  
A bill that the published proceedings identify as the business under debate during a speech.

**Bill mention**  
A reference to a bill in the text of a speech.

**Workspace note**  
A note by a workspace user about a source record or a policy topic.

**Policy file**  
An ongoing topic, bill, or other body of work that a staffer or team follows.

**Brief**  
A synthesis of source records and citations for a question or policy topic.
